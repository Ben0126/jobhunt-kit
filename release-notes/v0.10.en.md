- **One kit for the United States, Taiwan or both.** There is now one download (`jobhunt-kit-windows.zip`, `jobhunt-kit-mac-apple.zip`, `jobhunt-kit-mac-intel.zip`) instead of two editions. The first time, you choose where you are looking for work, in the chat or on the program's start screen (English by default, switchable to Chinese). You can add a market later, not remove one.
  - Looking in both: your experience is written up once, in English first. When a Taiwan application needs Chinese, the AI makes a Chinese card from the English one and you confirm it with 「確認中文 S-xxx」. Each job on the progress board says its country, and the board can show one country at a time.
- **Codex (ChatGPT) works too**, not only Claude. Open the folder in Codex and type "start". See "If you use ChatGPT" in START-HERE.md.
- **Download Word file.** The resume page saves your resume as an editable .docx, next to the PDF.
- **Taiwan resume photo.** Add a photo on the resume page. The program keeps it outside the job-search folder and removes its hidden details (camera, place); the AI never sees it.
- **Resume page:** preview the general version, switch templates, and look at the copies you sent. Each sent copy gets a version number such as V1.2.0 when you click "I applied, save a copy".
- **Add a job:** source, contact, status, dates, next step and notes in one go.
- **Jobs tab (United States):** open internships and new-grad jobs for every field, from SimplifyJobs and companies' own career pages, with each employer's public H-1B filing record (facts only, no ratings). A job you track is flagged when its posting closes.
- **US resume:** Skills & Certifications now come right after the summary, above Education.
- **Update now:** from this version on, the update reminder has a button that replaces the program and the AI rules and keeps your files.
- Versions up to v0.9 cannot update themselves to this one. Set up a new folder with the new download and keep your old folder as it is; see "Already using an older version?" below.
