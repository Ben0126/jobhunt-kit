- **New "Start here" tab.** The first time you open the program it shows five steps in the order people really work: tell the AI what you have done, confirm each card, look at your resume, find a job and tune your resume for it, write down that you applied. Each step has a sentence you can copy and say to the AI, and shows how far you are. A folder that already has confirmed experience or tracked jobs still opens on Progress.
- **US resume: Certifications and Training are now the last section.** Skills keeps Software and Languages. The Certifications line prints without a label (the heading already says it); a Training line keeps its label.
  - New folders get this automatically. A folder you already use keeps its old layout until you upgrade it: ask the AI to "update my resume layout", or run `jobhunt resume-sections` yourself. It first lists the exact lines it would change and changes nothing; only `jobhunt resume-sections --yes` writes, and it keeps a copy of the old file in `.jobhunt`.
  - The Taiwan (Chinese) resume is unchanged.
- Your files are never replaced by the update button. If you update from v0.10, the program and the AI rules change; your resume data changes only if you run the step above.
